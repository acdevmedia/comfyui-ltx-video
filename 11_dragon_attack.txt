A continuous photorealistic low wide shot in a medieval castle courtyard on a bright cloudy afternoon: grey stone battlements, a gatehouse with a portcullis, maroon banners with a gold lion and market stalls with canvas awnings, soft white daylight mixed with orange firelight on the right. A huge dark-red winged dragon flies above the courtyard, breathing a roaring stream of orange fire into a wooden watchtower on the walls, which bursts into flames and flying debris. Townspeople and guards in steel helmets and maroon tabards run in every direction. In the left foreground, a stout guard in a steel helmet, chainmail and maroon tabard stands with his back to the camera, sword drawn; just beyond him a younger guard in a matching tabard runs past. The camera holds steady. The young guard shouts in a cracking English voice, "Dragon! Everyone inside!" The foreground guard bellows in a West Country accent, "No! Not inside! Everyone stand by the tower!" The young guard stops. "Why, Geoffrey?" "I've sold the east side to the dragon. Seven gold a peasant." "You sold us?" "Not you, Colin. You're management." The dragon stops breathing fire, lands on the battlements beside the burning tower and turns its huge head towards Geoffrey. In a deep, irritated English voice it says, "You promised forty. That's thirty-one." Geoffrey spreads his arms. "Shrinkage. Some of them were fast." The dragon narrows its eyes. "I'm reporting you to the guild." It launches off the wall and flies away over the battlements. Geoffrey sheathes his sword and mutters, "Bloody dragons. No loyalty." Crackling timber, wind and fading screams fill the courtyard. No music.
