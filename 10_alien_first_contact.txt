EXT. ALIEN RAINFOREST – TWILIGHT

A continuous, photorealistic science-fiction shot in a dense alien rainforest under a deep violet twilight sky. Towering trees with smooth dark trunks rise over broad translucent leaves, clusters of blue and turquoise glowing plants light the damp forest floor, and thin mist drifts between huge roots. Soft violet skylight comes from above through gaps in the canopy, and the plants' cool blue glow lights everything from below, giving the clearing even, gentle light with soft shadows and visible detail in the darker trees. A human explorer in her early thirties, with short dark hair visible through the clear visor of her helmet, wears a grey expedition suit with a compact breathing pack and holds a handheld scanner in her left hand. Her face is clearly lit through the visor by the soft glow of the plants, with natural skin tones. The camera begins in a steady medium-wide shot as she crouches beside a fallen tree, holds the scanner towards a glowing plant and watches its small screen.

A twig snaps among the trees. She stands slowly, lowers the scanner and looks towards a shadowed gap between two enormous roots. A tall, slender alien steps cautiously out of the shadows, standing well above her height, with smooth pale-blue skin, a narrow elongated head, two large dark eyes and four long thin arms, the lower pair held close to its chest. It sets its long feet carefully on the damp ground as it walks a few slow steps into the clearing.

The camera moves slowly sideways into a medium two-shot, the explorer on the left and the alien on the right, a few paces apart. She raises her empty right hand, palm outward, and speaks softly, her calm voice slightly muffled by the helmet, "I'm not here to hurt you." The alien tilts its head and stares at her hand. After a pause, it slowly raises one upper arm and spreads its long fingers, copying her gesture, while its other three arms stay still. Her eyebrows lift and a small smile spreads across her face as she lowers the scanner to her side.

The alien takes one careful step closer and stops. The glowing plants around them brighten, casting blue light across her visor and its pale skin. The camera settles into a steady medium-wide shot of the two figures facing each other across the clearing.

The sound is a dense layer of alien wildlife: distant clicking insects, soft calls from unseen creatures, water dripping from leaves and rustling foliage. The alien breathes with a low, resonant hum and says no words. No music.
