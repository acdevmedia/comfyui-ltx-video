A continuous photorealistic medium-wide shot in a dense alien rainforest at violet twilight: huge dark mossy trees, mist drifting between distant pillar-like rock towers, two pale moons in a purple and pink sky, and clusters of glowing blue mushrooms and plants lighting the undergrowth. A young female explorer with dark curly hair inside a clear bubble helmet, wearing a grey expedition suit with a backpack and holding a handheld scanner, stands facing a tall, thin grey-blue alien with a long, smooth, backswept head, dark eyes and long spindly arms; they hold their raised palms up towards each other. The camera holds steady with a very slow push-in. In a warm, friendly English voice she says, "I'm Captain Harris. I come in peace." The alien replies in precise, deep, resonant English, "That's what the last one said. He ate our ambassador." She nods brightly. "Yeah, he left a review. Four stars. Bit chewy." The alien slowly lowers its hand. "You rated our ambassador?" "We rate everything, mate." She lifts the scanner and glances at the screen. "Your planet's on three." "Three what?" "Out of five. For a planet, that's bulldozers." The alien takes a step back, its long fingers trembling. "Do you come in peace?" She smiles sweetly. "Mostly in bulldozers." She taps the scanner. "Don't worry, you'll get a voucher." The glowing mushrooms dim one by one as distant creatures go quiet and water drips from the leaves. No music.
