A photorealistic science-fiction disaster sequence over a vast rocky desert on an alien planet, with rust-red sand, dark jagged mountains, long dry valleys and a pale yellow, dusty sky with two small moons high above the horizon. A high, bright sun shines through the dust haze, which softens the shadows and lights the landscape and the ship's hull clearly, with warm, natural colours and good detail in the shaded rock. An extreme wide shot shows a damaged spacecraft about the size of an airliner streaking diagonally down across the sky towards the desert: a broad, angular grey hull with two swept wings, a blunt cockpit at the front and three large engines at the rear. Its right-hand engine sputters orange flame and trails a long plume of black smoke while the other two burn steadily; the ship rolls slightly to the right, then levels out with short bursts from small thrusters along its hull. A hard cut transitions to a close tracking shot alongside the grey ship's left flank as it dives, the scorched hull filling the frame, heat haze shimmering behind the engines, black smoke streaming back from the burning engine and loose panels tearing away; the roar of the wind and the stuttering engine carry across the cut, and the desert rushes past below. The nose lifts, the front landing gear drops down, and dust starts swirling up from the ground under the exhausts. A hard cut transitions to a wide, ground-level shot looking down a long, flat valley between two rocky ridges; the grey spacecraft drops into frame from the upper left, hits the sand at a shallow angle with a thunderous impact, and its front landing gear folds. The ship skids along the valley floor throwing up a huge wall of sand and stones, its left wingtip clips a low rock outcrop and snaps off, and the hull grinds on, slowing until it stops in the middle of the valley. The engines wind down with a falling whine and their glow fades out, and dust drifts slowly past the still, battered ship as the sound fades to quiet desert wind. No music.
