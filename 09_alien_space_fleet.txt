A continuous photorealistic extreme wide shot in space above a vast blue Earth-like planet streaked with swirling white storm clouds, a field of grey asteroids and rocky debris drifting across the foreground, and a bright white sun blazing in the upper right against black, star-filled space. Six angular, battle-scarred grey human warships fly in formation with glowing blue engines: the enormous lead warship in the centre, one escort below it on the left and four further away; hard white sunlight picks out every panel and antenna. The camera slowly pushes towards the lead warship as asteroids tumble past. Over clear command radio, a nervous young male officer with a shaky voice says, "Admiral, the catering division has seized deck nine. They're demanding an apology for what you did to the chef." A stern female admiral answers in a crisp English voice, "I only put him out of the airlock a little bit." "He was out there for nine minutes, ma'am." "He came back." "He came back different, ma'am. He talks to the soup now." A pause. "And what does the soup say?" The officer swallows audibly. "It says you're next." Long silence on the radio. The admiral says calmly, "Target deck nine. All weapons." "Ma'am, that's our own ship!" "And not one person on it can make soup." Another silence. "And open the airlock again. He's looking at me."
