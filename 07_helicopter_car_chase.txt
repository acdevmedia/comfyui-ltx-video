A continuous photorealistic low tracking shot on a sunny late afternoon along an industrial road in Los Angeles, the downtown skyline and a concrete overpass ahead, parked lorries along the roadside, warm low sunlight from the left glinting off the cars. A red mid-engine supercar speeds straight towards the camera with a dark grey muscle car close on its tail and a black police helicopter flying low just above them; the camera travels backwards in front of the red car, keeping all three in frame. A dark-haired man grips the wheel of the red car. His voice shouts from inside in an American accent, "Babe, quick question. Why is somebody banging on the inside of the front trunk?" A woman's voice answers calmly from the passenger seat, "That's my husband." "Is he alive?" "He's fine. He's sulking." Muffled thumping comes from the front of the car. Gunshots crack from the grey car behind and sparks flash off the road. He yells, "Who the hell is that?" She sighs. "His brothers. They think you kidnapped him." "I didn't kidnap him!" "Well, technically, you're driving." Over the police radio, a calm female pilot with a flat American voice says, "Control, the trunk is now screaming. Requesting a bigger helicopter." The thumping gets louder. The woman leans forward and yells at the dashboard, "Gary, if you don't stop that, I'm keeping the dog!" The thumping stops instantly. Engines roar, tyres hiss and the rotors thud overhead. No music.
