A continuous photorealistic wide aerial shot high above a bright sea of white clouds under a deep blue sky: the vast underside of a colossal ridged dark-silver alien disc, dotted with lit windows, fills the top of the frame, its enormous hangar doors hanging open to reveal a long lit hangar inside. A matte-black flying-wing stealth bomber glides just below the disc, having just left the hangar, direct sunlight glinting on its edges. The camera flies alongside the bomber, keeping it and the disc in frame. Over a crisp military radio, a British female controller says in a tight, clipped voice, "Unidentified aircraft, state your country of origin." A cheerful male pilot with a West Midlands accent replies, "Technically, Wolverhampton." The controller snaps, "You just flew out of an alien spacecraft!" "Yeah. I sold them the other one." A pause. "You sold a British stealth bomber to aliens?" "Two for one, love. Bought this one back on finance." Her voice rises: "Who authorised this?" "Same bloke who authorised the first one. Me. After six pints." "This is treason!" "It's entrepreneurship. And they threw in a probe voucher." "A what?" A long beat of radio static. "Don't ask about the voucher." The hangar doors begin to close behind the bomber as it drifts away from the disc. The pilot adds quietly, "Seriously. Don't ask." Engine roar and high-altitude wind continue. No music.
